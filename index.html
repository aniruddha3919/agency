Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in each plan?
All plans include professional content creation (Reels/Shorts and Carousel posts), content strategy, posting schedule management, and a FREE custom website. Higher tiers offer more content volume, priority support, and advanced analytics.
How long does it take to see results?
Most clients see initial engagement within the first 2 weeks. Significant growth in followers and website traffic typically occurs within 30-60 days with consistent posting. Sales and booking conversions depend on your product/service and market, but we optimize continuously.
Do I need to provide content ideas?
No! We handle everything from content ideation to scripting to production. You just approve the final content before posting. However, if you have specific ideas or campaigns, we're happy to incorporate them into the strategy.
Can I cancel or change plans?
Yes, plans are flexible. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Cancellations require 30 days notice. We recommend committing to at least 3 months for best results, but there are no long-term contracts.
What if I already have a website?
No problem! We can optimize your existing website for conversions, integrate booking/payment systems, or build a new one from scratch. The FREE website in each plan can be a redesign, landing page, or completely new site based on your needs.
Do you offer refunds?
We stand behind our work. If you're not satisfied within the first 15 days, we'll work with you to make it right. Refunds are considered on a case-by-case basis for the first month only, after content delivery review.
